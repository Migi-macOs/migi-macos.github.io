Native Prompt Manager for Mac

Migi is a native macOS app for managing fill-in-the-blank templates for ChatGPT and Claude. Press ⌥ Space from anywhere to access your prompt library in a floating window. Fill placeholders visually, autocomplete suggests as you type, then copy instantly to your clipboard.

Instant Access Everywhere

Global hotkey brings up your templates immediately. No digging through apps or notes. Built in SwiftUI for speed and native feel. Everything stays local with SwiftData—no cloud, no tracking.

Smart Placeholders and Autocomplete

Fill-in-the-blank templates with visual management. Autocomplete tracks placeholders across your prompt. Reference them multiple times effortlessly. Syntax checking prevents errors before copy.

Organized and Searchable

Folder-based organization. Fuzzy search understands intent, not just keywords. Perfect for prompt engineering, code snippets, marketing copy, docs, or support responses.

Frictionless Workflow

Reduces chaos of copying repetitive prompts. From thought to execution in seconds. Focus on results, not repetition.



How It Works

1. Press ⌥ Space

Global hotkey opens your prompt library from any app.

2. Select and Fill

Choose a template. Autocomplete and visual placeholders guide you as you type.

3. Copy and Paste

Hit Enter. Prompt is on clipboard, ready for ChatGPT or Claude.

4. Organize and Reuse

Build folders, search fuzzily, refine templates over time.

macOS compatibility?
